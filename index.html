01 / THE PROJECT

Prevent errors before they become delays.

A realistic logistics process improvement proposal, inspired by professional experience. The project aims to reduce avoidable customs holds and manual customer follow-ups by collecting required invoices before shipping labels are generated.

12 weeksProposed schedule
AED 45,000Illustrative budget
1 warehouseControlled pilot
50%Missing invoice reduction target

Current process

Missing invoices require staff to contact customers, potentially delaying processing or leading to returns.

Proposed process

Identify eligible shipments, require invoice upload or generation, validate required fields, securely store documents, and generate the label.

01Executive summary
Documentation errors create avoidable customs holds, customer follow-up and shipment returns.
Proposed solution
Require invoice upload or generation before label creation for eligible online shipments, validate
required fields and store documents securely for authorized staff.
Success measure                                   Baseline / target
Average processing delay                          5% reduction
On-time processing                                85% to 90%

10Pilot and measurement plan
Before deployment
• Validate historical baselines and eligible online shipment denominator.
• Confirm documentation rules, security testing and release approval.
• Train staff and establish support and rollback procedures.
During the two-week pilot
• Track missing invoices / eligible online shipments, on-time processing and average delay daily.
• Monitor booking abandonment, incorrect invoices and operational disruptions.
• Compare like-for-like baseline and pilot cohorts; record volume and mix changes.
Decision gate: sponsor reviews measured results, compliance, customer adoption and stability
before approving any separate expansion.

13Lessons and portfolio index
• Prevent documentation errors upstream rather than relying on reactive follow-up.
• Engage IT, operations, customer service and compliance early.
• Pilot before scaling; validate baseline and customer impact.
